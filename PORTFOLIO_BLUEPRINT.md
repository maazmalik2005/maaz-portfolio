1. Personal Brand Identity
Brand name: Maaz Sohail
Brand descriptor: Digital Marketing Specialist | SEO Expert | WordPress Developer
Brand essence: Technical depth meets marketing strategy. Most marketers cannot touch the website, and most developers do not think about search or conversion. Maaz works across both, so growth work is not blocked by hand-offs.
Brand personality: Precise, calm, analytical, quietly confident, modern, reliable.
Brand promise: Websites and campaigns built so the right customers can find a business, trust it and contact it.
Brand voice
• First person, direct and plain-spoken.
• Short sentences, concrete verbs (build, audit, optimise, manage, plan).
• No hype words such as "guru", "ninja", "rockstar", "guaranteed #1 rankings".
• Confident about process, honest about outcomes.
Core brand pillars
1. Visibility: SEO, local search and technical foundations.
2. Presence: WordPress and modern websites that look credible and load fast.
3. Growth: Social and paid campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok and Snapchat.
4. Technical trust: A security-aware, IT-literate approach to every site and account.
Taglines (choose one for the footer and meta usage)
• "Strategy, search and websites that work together." (recommended)
• "Technical know-how for digital growth."
• "Be found. Be trusted. Be chosen."
Logo direction: Text-based monogram "MS" in a rounded square or a clean wordmark "Maaz Sohail" in Space Grotesk, accent #6366F1 on the initials. No mascots or clip-art.
2. Professional Positioning
Positioning statement
Maaz Sohail is a freelance digital marketing and web specialist who combines SEO, WordPress development and paid and social campaign management with an IT and cybersecurity background, helping small and mid-sized businesses build a credible, visible and well-maintained online presence.
Target clients
• Local and regional service businesses (for example in the UAE and Pakistan).
• E-commerce stores that need website management plus marketing support.
• Startups and small companies launching or rebuilding a website.
Differentiators
• One person covering strategy, SEO, website build and campaign setup.
• Technical SEO and site performance handled by someone who can edit the site directly.
• IT-security awareness applied to website hygiene and account management.
• Remote-first workflow with clear communication.
Positioning rules for all copy
• Lead with what the client gains, then how.
• Present about two years of hands-on freelance experience as focused and current, never as junior and never inflated.
• Separate proven professional skills from technologies currently being learned.
Target search identities: Digital Marketing Specialist, SEO Expert, WordPress Developer, Freelance Digital Marketer, Remote Digital Marketing Specialist, Website Management Specialist.

• Badges and chips: small pills, 12px text. Type badges use accent (Client), cyan (Practice), neutral grey (Concept).
• Icons: one consistent line-icon family, 1.5px stroke, in accent or cyan.
• Forms: dark inputs with subtle borders, visible labels, clear error text beneath fields.
Motion (recommendations only)
• Gentle fade and 12 to 16px upward reveal when sections enter the viewport, 300 to 500ms, once only.
• Card hover lift and border change, 200ms.
• Nav background change on scroll.
• Staggered entrance for the hero text.
• Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling all non-essential animation.
• Avoid parallax, typing effects, bouncing elements, particle backgrounds and auto-playing carousels.
Accessibility
• Semantic headings (one H1), landmarks, skip-to-content link.
• Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements.
• Alt text on all images, labels on all form fields.
• Colour is never the only indicator of state.
Responsive behaviour: mobile-first. Breakpoints near 640px, 768px, 1024px and 1280px. Stack columns on mobile, keep CTAs full-width on small screens, ensure no horizontal scroll.
Performance goals: optimised images in modern formats, lazy-loaded below the fold, fonts limited to the three listed families with swap loading, minimal client-side scripting, strong Core Web Vitals (a good proof of the technical SEO message).
Avoid: excessive gradients, neon glows, clutter, cartoon illustrations, emoji in headings, stock-photo handshakes, long unbroken text walls.
